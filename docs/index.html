AISCO - FOCUS PROJECT

AI for Solar Car Optimisation

Artificial Intelligence for Solar Car Optimisation — a focus project of aCentauri Solar Racing and ETH Zürich's IDEAL Lab.

AISCO revolutionises the design process of solar race cars by exploring a diverse range of aeroshells, estimating drag and solar performance, and ranking generated concepts by minimal race time.

The system combines a self-built CFD-AI, geometry-aware optimisation, and street-legal packaging constraints to accelerate engineering decisions while preserving performance realism.

Projects

Aeroshell Optimiser

Maximises race performance through weighted aerodynamic and solar losses. Built on Geometry Informed Neural Networks and Wavelet Implicit Neural Representations.

Assembler

Bridges regulations, packaging boundaries, visibility standards, and internal components into buildable, compliant car geometry.

CFD AI

Replaces repeated high-fidelity Reynolds-averaged Navier–Stokes simulations with near-instant drag prediction used directly in optimisation.

GUI

Gives engineers practical control of constraints, loss weights, and regulation parameters through a simple, transparent interface.

Solar Estimator

Estimates geometry-dependent solar input from incidence, weather, deck incline, cell placement, and string arrangement for direct optimisation feedback.

LLM | aCeBot

Internal knowledge assistant trained on project documentation and team history to support knowledge transfer and reduce repeated mistakes.
